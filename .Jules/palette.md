## 2026-05-13 - Expandable Menus Accessibility
**Learning:** Ensure the trigger button has 'aria-expanded' AND 'aria-controls', and that the expanded panel has a matching 'id', 'role="region"', and 'aria-labelledby' linking back to the trigger.
**Action:** Adding missing 'aria-controls' to the buttons and matching IDs to their corresponding 'ul' blocks in tree navigation elements for better accessibility.
